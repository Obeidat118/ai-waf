Graduation Project — AI-Powered WAF

A firewall that learns what an attack looks like.

AI-WAF inspects every request in two stages — first flagging anything abnormal, then classifying exactly what it is (SQL Injection, XSS, DDoS, Brute Force, Bot) — and gives you the logs, alerts, and reports to prove it.

01 — Layer One

Normal vs. Malicious

A Random Forest classifier screens every request in real time, tuned for speed over the 2-second budget.

02 — Layer Two

Attack-type classification

Flagged traffic goes to a one-vs-rest SVM that names the attack: SQLi, XSS, DDoS, Brute Force, or Bot.

03 — Dashboard

Logs, alerts, reports

Every decision is written to the database and surfaced live, with severity-ranked alerts and summary reports.
